Blue Insights — designed for trustworthy AI at deployment scale

This page sits alongside the Blue Insights — FY26 Revenue prototype. It captures the research context behind the concept and how it maps to the work of Tomoro.ai, the AI engineering firm OpenAI has agreed to acquire as the founding company of its "OpenAI Deployment Company" (announced May 2026, subject to regulatory approval).

Why this prototype speaks to that work

Production-ready, not demo-ware

The Research sidekick is designed around the realities of enterprise deployment: permissioned data sources, a restricted-access state with a request flow, and a share step that forces human review of sensitive figures before anything leaves the tool.

Explainability as the trust mechanism

Every claim carries a citation, a "Why?" trace, and an explicit split into evidence / assumption / unknown. That is the interaction pattern enterprises need before they let an agent near board-level numbers.

Human-in-the-loop correction

The user can challenge an assumption and the agent re-runs with the correction applied — a shared-goal loop ("an answer you can defend") rather than a one-shot answer box. Adoption depends on this feeling of joint work.

Systemised, deployable design

The whole prototype runs on a tokenised design system (colour, type, spacing, AI-semantic states like accountability and transparency), so the pattern can be re-skinned and shipped inside a client's product quickly — the 12-week cadence Tomoro works to.

The concept in one line

An analyst insight platform where the AI agent shows its working — evidence, assumptions and unknowns — so enterprise teams can defend the numbers it gives them.
