About this concept

Blue Insights is a working prototype I built to test a question: what does an AI research assistant need to show before a person in a regulated financial environment can defend its answer?

The dashboard is deliberately ordinary — revenue by quarter, a dip in Q3, an annotation inviting you to ask why. The interesting part is what happens when you do. The Research sidekick doesn't hand back a paragraph; it separates what it found into evidence, assumptions and unknowns, names the sources it was permitted to read, flags the one it wasn't, and grades its own confidence accordingly. When it can't confirm something, it says so and shows the request-access path instead of guessing.

The patterns I was experimenting with: shared goals between the person and the agent, working shown by default, data-permission boundaries treated as a first-class UI concern, checks you can save and re-run, and a human review step before anything is exported. None of the numbers are real — the data is invented for the demo. The design problems aren't.

Built by Elise Pace as a personal experiment in AI-enabled designer workflows. Everything here — interface, interaction model, microcopy — is my own work.
